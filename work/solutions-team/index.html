Building the solutions team at Wati

When I joined Wati in May 2024, the solutions team was still taking shape. I helped set it up: how the team works, how it documents solutions and how deals move to delivery.

Type
Team and process design
Status
Live and in use
Sector
Wati solutions team
Built with
Notion

The problem

The solutions team was loosely organised. Each deal was handled in its own way, and there was no written playbook, shared documentation or agreed way to hand a deal over to delivery.

What I built

  • The team setup and working instructions: who does what from discovery to handover.
  • A roadmap for how the solutions function would grow.
  • Notion documentation the team works from, including solution strategies for common customer needs.
  • The presales playbook.
  • The presales to delivery handover process, so delivery starts from a written, agreed scope.

Key decisions

  • Write everything down in one place the whole team uses, instead of relying on what each person remembers.
  • Make the handover a document delivery can build from, not a call.
  • Keep two versions of every solution: a clean proposal for the customer and an internal list of caveats and open questions.

Result

The team runs on the playbook, documentation and handover process, and delivery teams build from the handover. I set up the structure, wrote the instructions, documentation and playbook, and designed the handover process.
