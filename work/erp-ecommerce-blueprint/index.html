ERP and ecommerce solution strategy

A solution strategy for a jewellery retailer: Shopify, a custom ERP and an AI agent on WhatsApp, scoped requirement by requirement before anything was promised.

Type
Solution strategy
Status
Designed and scoped
Sector
Jewellery retail
Built with
Shopify, custom ERP

The problem

Customer and order data sat in two systems. Before signing, both sides needed to know what was standard, what needed custom work and what it would cost.

What I built

  • A requirements map across Shopify, the custom ERP and the AI agent.
  • Each requirement labelled as out of the box, needing a workaround, depending on the customer's systems, or a real gap.
  • Implementation hours and support packs costed up front.

Key decisions

  • Label every requirement before promising anything, so the proposal matched what the product could do.
  • Cost implementation and ongoing support separately, so pricing reflected the real effort.
  • Keep Shopify and the ERP as the systems of record and connect them to WhatsApp, rather than copying data into a new store.

Result

A scoping document and a costed implementation plan the sales team could price and the delivery team could build from. My part: discovery, solution architecture, scoping and costing.
